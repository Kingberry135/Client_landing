THE REAL PROBLEM

It Is Not Always That Opportunities Are Missing. Sometimes, You Simply Don't Know Where to Look.

Many people spend months searching for employment, scrolling through social media, applying for opportunities and waiting for somebody to notice them. Yet businesses, professionals and organisations constantly need help with everyday digital tasks.

Waiting for Opportunities

You keep waiting for a vacancy, an employer or somebody to offer you work. But have you learned how to identify people who already need a service?

Using Your Device Without a Plan

Your phone can connect you to businesses, information, potential customers and useful tools. The important question is whether you know how to turn those resources into action.

Not Knowing Where Clients Are

Having a useful skill is one thing. Knowing who needs it, where to find them, how to approach them and how to secure a project is another.

Here is the shift: Instead of only asking, "Where can I find a job?", learn to ask, "Who needs a service I can provide, and how can I reach them?"

COMMON CONCERNS

You May Be Asking Yourself...

"I don't have a laptop."

Some research and communication activities can be done with a smartphone. However, some spreadsheet and project tasks are more suitable for a computer. We explain the practical requirements so you can plan accordingly.

"I don't know how to find clients."

That is one of the central things this training is designed to teach: where to research, how to identify prospects and how to approach them.

"I am not an expert."

You do not need to pretend to be an expert. You need to learn the service, practise, understand your limits and only accept work you can competently deliver.

"Do I need Fiverr or Upwork?"

No. The client-discovery strategy focuses on finding and approaching potential clients directly. You can explore platforms separately if you choose.
